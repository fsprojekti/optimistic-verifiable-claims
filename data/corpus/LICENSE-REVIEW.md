# Corpus licensing review

**Status:** source-model licensing resolved; slicer/profile freeze still pending.

Every source model must permit redistribution of the archived model and the generated G-code. A
download being free of charge is not evidence of permission. The final manifest must record the
license, attribution, source URL, immutable upstream revision, original hash, output hash, and any
share-alike or corresponding-source obligation.
